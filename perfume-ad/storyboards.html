Before the build

Two things to settle

  1. What "4K camera tracking" means here. I read it as a 4K render where the virtual camera locks onto and follows the moving objects (orbit, follow, focus pulls). True camera tracking, where CG is matched into filmed footage, needs footage, so it only applies to Board C with real or generated video.
  2. Render time. The 10s version took about 5½ minutes at 1080p here. 4K has four times the pixels and the film is twice as long, so expect roughly 45–90 minutes per full render (estimate). I'll check still frames at 4K before committing to a full pass.
