# MOTION.md — ORVELLE

> Written 2026-11-25 from the brief, not from reference frames (none were supplied for this fictitious brand). Where a real brand would need its owner's call, the note says ASK ME.

## 1. Colours
| Hex | Name | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `#07040A` | Night | Background, fog, floor. Most of every frame. |
| `#2A0F1F` | Plum | Atmosphere: the haze behind the bottle. Never on text. |
| `#F2A33A` | Saffron | The one accent: the liquid and the key light. One glow source per frame, and it is this. |
| `#E8C27A` | Gold | Metal (collar, cap band), hairlines, small caps, the wordmark. |
| `#F6EAD6` | Cream | Headline and body text. |

## 2. Type
- Display: Cormorant Garamond 300, italic 300 for the hook. Wordmark 130px, tracking 28px.
- Hook: italic 120px.
- Labels: Inter 300/400, all caps, 22–28px, tracking 9–12px.
- Text is a solid colour. No gradients on text.

## 3. Timing
- In: 0.7–0.9s, ease-out cubic, fade plus a 24px rise. Tracking settles from wide to set while it rises.
- Hold: at least 1.2s for any line; the end card holds 2.4s.
- Out: 0.5s, ease-in-out, fade with a slight blur.
- Stagger letters 0.07s, lines 0.4s.

## 4. Movement
- 30fps. One continuous camera move for the whole film: a slow pull back and a gentle arc (no more than 45°), settling by 8.8s. No cuts, shakes or spins.
- Easing: cubic in-out for the camera, cubic out for things arriving. No overshoot or bounce.
- Handmade feel: gold dust and saffron threads drift in depth with seeded, slightly different speeds; the key light sweeps across the glass once.

## 5. Texture and finish
Real glass (transmission, clearcoat), polished gold, black lacquer cap. A dark mirror floor with a dim reflection. Subtle bloom on highlights only. Soft vignette. Ground under the title stays dark.

## 6. Never
1. Never bounce, overshoot or use elastic easing.
2. Never put gradients on text or use typewriter reveals.
3. Never place text outside the Reel safe zone: y 360–1420, and not in the right 120px.
4. Never show a price, claim, review or number that is not in brand.md.
5. Never use more than one glow source, or let bloom blow out the glass.

## 7. Done right (10s, 1080×1920)
- 0–3s: macro on the cap and gold collar, saffron threads and dust drifting past, the key light sweeping. "Some nights / are made to be remembered" rises in, holds, leaves.
- 3–7s: the camera keeps pulling back and arcs; the full bottle is revealed. Three note cards (Saffron, Amber, Oud) float past in depth.
- 7–10s: the camera settles frontally, the bottle in the upper half. ORVELLE lands letter by letter, then "Midnight Saffron", "Eau de Parfum", and "Discover the night". Holds to the end.
- Sound: low warm bed, a soft hit on each new beat, a rising shimmer under the last line.
